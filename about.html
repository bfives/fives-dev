Helping people move forward.

Fives Dev exists to make important next steps feel simpler, clearer and more possible.

What we do

Fives Dev is a South African platform that helps young people move forward. We provide practical services, digital support and useful resources for people building their education, careers, skills, businesses and everyday lives.

From CVs and university applications to iPhone repairs and professional websites, Fives Dev brings reliable, affordable support into one place. We also share selected tools and resources that make important decisions easier.

Why Fives Dev exists

Starting something new can be confusing. Whether you are applying for university, looking for work, building your first CV, learning a skill, fixing your phone or starting a business, it is not always clear where to begin.

Fives Dev exists to make those next steps simpler. We combine practical services with clear guidance, so young South Africans can spend less time figuring things out and more time moving forward.

Who we help

Our main focus is young people in South Africa: students, graduates, job seekers, young professionals and aspiring entrepreneurs. We also support small businesses that need accessible digital services.

Wherever you are in your journey, still in school, studying, looking for your first opportunity, growing a side hustle or running a business, Fives Dev is here to offer practical support for the next step.

How we work

We keep things clear, straightforward and human. You should understand what a service includes, what you need to provide, what it costs and what happens next.

We avoid unnecessary jargon and complicated processes, while making sure you can get real support when you need it.

Our goal

Our goal is simple: to help people move forward.

That could mean submitting an application, improving a CV, gaining a new skill, finding an opportunity, repairing a phone or building a professional online presence for a business.

As Fives Dev grows, we will continue building useful services and resources around the real needs of young people in South Africa.
