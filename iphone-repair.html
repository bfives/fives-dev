FAQ

Common questions

How long does a repair take?+

Most screen and battery repairs are completed on the same day, usually within 2 to 4 hours once we have the device.

Do you use genuine parts?+

We use high-grade OEM-equivalent parts that match Apple specs. Genuine Apple parts are available on request at an additional cost.

What if I'm not sure what's wrong?+

No problem, tick the "I'm not sure" option when booking. Our technicians will diagnose it and quote you before any work is done.

Is there a warranty?+

Yes, every repair carries a 14-day workmanship warranty covering the specific part we replaced.

How do I pay?+

Direct bank transfer (EFT), once we've confirmed your quote. See payment details →
