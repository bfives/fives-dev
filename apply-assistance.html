What's covered, and what isn't

Institution fees are separate

Our fee only covers our help with filling in and submitting your application. You're still responsible for paying the university's own application fee directly to them.

NSFAS

Financial aid if you need help funding your studies. Just mention it when you message us or fill in the form, and we'll guide you through it.

Funza Lushaka

The bursary for students studying towards a B.Ed (Bachelor of Education). Same, just mention it and we'll help.

FAQ

Questions parents & learners ask

Do I still pay the university's own application fee?+

Yes. Most public universities charge R100 to R250 per application. That's separate from our service fee and is paid directly to the institution.

What if I haven't written matric yet?+

That's fine, we can apply using your Grade 11 final results, then update your file once your Grade 12 mid-year and final results are released.

Which documents do I need?+

Your ID document, latest school report or matric results, and proof of address. We'll tell you if a specific institution needs anything extra.

Do you help with NSFAS or bursaries?+

Yes, just mention it when you message us or fill in the form, and we'll guide you through the NSFAS or Funza Lushaka process.
