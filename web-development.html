Questions

Frequently asked.

How does the booking process work?+

Choose a package, complete the checkout form with your project details, and follow the payment instructions. Once payment reflects, we schedule your kickoff call within 24 hours.

How long does a project take?+

Starter sites go live in 3–5 business days, Business sites in 7–10 days, and E-Commerce stores in 10–14 days. Custom projects are scoped individually.

Do you help with domain registration and hosting?+

Yes. Fives Dev can register your domain and set up professional hosting on your behalf, or guide you through doing it yourself, the domain is always registered in your name.

What if I need changes after launch?+

Small tweaks are usually quick and quoted fairly. For ongoing changes, we offer optional monthly care plans so your site stays fresh and up to date.

How do I pay?+

We use direct bank transfer (EFT) for now. After checkout, you'll receive payment instructions with a unique Order ID to use as your reference.

Do you sign an agreement?+

Yes. Every project starts with a short written scope and terms so both sides are protected and clear on deliverables.

Ready to build something you're proud of?

Book your project in a couple of minutes. Share your details, pay by EFT, and we'll be in touch within 24 hours to kick things off.
